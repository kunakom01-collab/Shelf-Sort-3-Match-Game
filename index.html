1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ShelfIQ 3D ("the App", package ID com.aistudio.shelfiq3d.kmpzvx) handles information. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

We do not operate user accounts or collect personally identifiable information (PII) on our own servers. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or login credentials
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Photos, videos, microphone audio, contacts, or personal files

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide core gameplay functionality, ShelfIQ 3D stores limited non-personal app settings and game progress exclusively on your local device using Android Preferences DataStore:

  • Campaign Progress: Your highest unlocked level (for sequential stage progression).
  • App Preferences: Your chosen theme mode (light/dark), category filter selection, and Live Strategy Advisor visibility toggle.
  • Session Gameplay Metrics: Round scores, move counts, combo streaks, and strategy progress computed locally during gameplay.

This local data never leaves your device unless you have enabled standard Android OS system backups on your device (see Section 5).

4. Device Permissions

ShelfIQ 3D requests only standard install-time network permissions (INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) required to load ads and consent forms, plus Google Play Advertising ID access as declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or device storage.

5. Android System Backup

The App supports standard Android Auto Backup (android:allowBackup="true"). If you choose to enable Google/Android device backup in your Android system settings, your local app preferences and unlocked level progress may be backed up to your personal Google Drive backup strictly under Google's Android backup controls. You can disable this at any time in your device's system settings.

6. Third-Party Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

ShelfIQ 3D uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded video ads. When ads are served, Google and its certified ad technology partners may collect and process:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID) or app-set identifiers
  • IP address (used for coarse region estimation and invalid-traffic/fraud prevention)
  • Ad impressions, ad interaction events, and SDK performance diagnostics

Where required by law (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before initializing ads, and provides an in-app Ad Privacy & Consent Options setting in the Main Menu. You can also reset or limit ad personalization anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Apps.

7. Children's Privacy

ShelfIQ 3D is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all gameplay preferences and unlocked level data are stored locally on your device, you have full control over them. You can permanently delete all App data at any time by:

  • Going to Android Settings → Apps → ShelfIQ 3D → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the App from your device.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if new features or services are added in future releases. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
